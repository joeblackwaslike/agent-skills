# Free Domain with Pro

Every paid Pro team gets one free first-year custom domain. You choose an [eligible domain](#which-domains-tlds-are-eligible), Vercel covers the first year of registration, and the domain is managed alongside your other [Vercel-registered domains](/docs/domains/working-with-domains). The offer launched with new Pro subscriptions and now extends to all Pro teams.

## Which domains (TLDs) are eligible?

The offer only covers these top-level domains (TLDs):

- `.app`
- `.dev`
- `.online`
- `.site`
- `.space`
- `.store`
- `.tech`
- `.website`

Premium domains are not eligible for this offer, including premium domains that use one of the eligible TLDs above. For the full list of TLDs you can register and use on Vercel, see [Supported domains](/docs/domains/supported-domains).

## How do I know if the domain I want is eligible?

Go to [vercel.com/domains](https://vercel.com/domains) and search for the domain you want. If the domain shows **Free With Pro**, it is eligible for this offer. If it does not show **Free With Pro**, it is not eligible.

## How do I claim my free domain?

You can claim the free domain from your dashboard if your Pro team is eligible for the offer. Search for a domain that shows **Free With Pro**, select it, and complete checkout to register it.

## How long do I have to claim it?

The offer is available for a limited time. Eligible Pro teams can claim one free domain while the promotion is active. If the offer is no longer shown in your dashboard or a domain does not show **Free With Pro**, it is not eligible for the promotion.

## How many free domains do I get?

One free domain per eligible Pro team. Once a free domain is claimed, it is final. You cannot swap it for another domain or change the selected domain under this offer.

## What happens after the first year?

Vercel covers the first year of registration. After the first year, the domain renews at the regular price shown at checkout when you claim it. Renewals are automatic by default, and you can manage them from your dashboard. See [Managing domain renewals and redemptions](/docs/domains/working-with-domains/renew-a-domain).

## Am I eligible if I'm already on Pro or on a Pro trial?

Existing Pro teams may be eligible for this offer while the promotion is available. Pro trials are not eligible until the team upgrades to Pro. To check eligibility, visit your dashboard or search for a domain at [vercel.com/domains](https://vercel.com/domains). Eligible domains will show **Free With Pro**.
