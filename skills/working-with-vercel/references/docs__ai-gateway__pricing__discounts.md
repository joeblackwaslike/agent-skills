# AI Gateway Discounts

Discounts on [AI Gateway](/docs/ai-gateway) token spend come in two forms: models that are already priced below the provider's list price for every team, and [negotiated custom discounts](#volume-discounts).

## Discounted models

AI Gateway frequently offers models that cost less through AI Gateway than the provider's list price. These discounts apply automatically to all users when using the respective model-slug. [Browse the current promos](/ai-gateway/models?promo=true) to see which models are discounted or free today.

## Volume discounts

Custom discounts on token spend are available on [AI Gateway](/docs/ai-gateway) for volume commitments. Discounts vary by model and spend. Volume customers can also pay by invoice, which has no payment processing fees.

For more information, [contact our sales team](/contact/sales?content=I%27m%20interested%20in%20AI%20Gateway%20volume%20discounts) or email <ai-gateway@vercel.com>.

For standard pay-as-you-go rates, see [AI Gateway pricing](/docs/ai-gateway/pricing).
